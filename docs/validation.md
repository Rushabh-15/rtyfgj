## NESO comparison

[NESO's metric](https://www.neso.energy/document/377601/download): average absolute error between the day-ahead forecast published between 09:00 and 10:00 and post-event settlement-metered outturn, per half-hour, as a percentage of capacity for BM wind units, excluding sites with a bid-offer acceptance or that withdrew availability. January 2026: **4.99%** in NESO's January 2026 report; the previous-year row of the [2026-27 reports](https://www.neso.energy/document/382306/download) shows 4.9%.

| NESO published (exclusions applied, settlement metering) | 4.99% |

| Diagnostic only: % of capacity after removing each forecast's January mean error | 6.16% / 5.28% |

Caveats. 17 of 31 January days in NESO's archive carry a forecast timestamp of 4 June 2026 (the rows were reloaded), so they cannot be confirmed as the 09:00–10:00 forecast; on the other days the comparison gives 8.87% (WINDFOR) and 6.43% (NESO). NESO's forecast for 2026-01-28 was timestamped outside 09:00–10:00, matching the IT issue NESO reports for 27–28 January. FUELHH is operational metering; NESO uses settlement metering after the initial settlement run.
